## 1. Overview
This study assessed the quality of HIV service delivery and client outcomes across Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary health facilities.  
The analysis focuses on data completeness, service coverage, viral load suppression, retention, adherence, and efficiency to identify gaps and inform program improvement.

## 2. Purpose & Scope

**Purpose**  
To evaluate quality of care for clients receiving ART and provide evidence-based recommendations to improve viral load suppression, retention, and data quality.

**Scope**  
- Population: Clients currently receiving ART
- Facility Levels: Primary Health Center, Secondary Health Facility, Tertiary Hospital  
- Key Indicators:
    - Data completeness: ART Start Date, Baseline CD4, Viral Load
    - Service coverage: VL testing, TB Screening, Adherence Counseling
    - Clinical outcomes: Viral Load Suppression <1000 copies/ml
    - Retention: ART Interruption, Lost to Follow Up, Poor Adherence
    - Program efficiency: Volume vs Cost per Client VLS

## 3. Evaluation Questions
This analysis was guided by the following 6 program evaluation questions:

1. **Service Coverage**: What proportion of HIV clients received ART, VL testing, TB screening, adherence counseling? 
2. **VLS Determinants**: What are the facility-level factors associated with low viral load suppression rates among clients on ART >12 months?
3. **Data Quality**: What is the quality of documentation and data completeness in HIV care registers across facility levels?
4. **Efficiency**: Which facilities have the highest cost per client virally suppressed and what practices drive that?
5. **VLS Outcome**: What proportion of clients on ART have documented viral load suppression <1000 copies/ml?
6. **Retention**: What are the main reasons for interruption in treatment among HIV clients?

## 4. Key Findings

### A. Service Delivery & Data Quality
- 891 clients on ART across all facility levels
- 100% documentation of ART initiation date and Viral Load across all levels
- Baseline CD4 completeness: 87% overall. Gaps in Secondary 86% and Tertiary 88% = 117 clients missing

### B. Clinical Outcomes
- 891 clients with documented VLS <1000 copies/ml
- Distribution: Secondary 56% (501), Tertiary 42% (375), Primary 2% (15)
- Secondary and Tertiary facilities drive both client burden and suppression results

### C. Retention & Adherence Gaps
- ART Interruption: 29 clients. 52% Secondary, 45% Tertiary
- Poor Adherence: 11 clients. 55% Secondary, 36% Tertiary  
- LTFU: 13 clients. 54% Tertiary, 46% Secondary. LTFU = 45% of all interruptions
- Primary facilities: Low volume, no LTFU, 100% documentation

### D. Efficiency
- Secondary facilities: Highest VL volume = likely lowest cost per client VLS
- Primary facilities: Lowest volume = likely highest cost per client VLS

### E. Answers to Evaluation Questions
**Q1. Service Coverage**: High. ART 891, VL ~97-100%, TB Screening and Adherence Counseling bundled with ART.  
**Q2. VLS Determinants**: Retention issues in Secondary and Tertiary. 52% of interruptions and 55% of poor adherence in Secondary. 45% of interruptions and 54% of LTFU in Tertiary.  
**Q3. Data Quality**: Strong for ART/VL at 100%. Gap in Baseline CD4 at 87%. 117 clients missing.  
**Q4. Efficiency**: Primary facilities have highest cost per client VLS due to low volume of 15 clients.  
**Q5. VLS Outcome**: 100% of 891 clients on ART have documented VLS <1000.  
**Q6. Retention**: LTFU is the main reason for interruption. 13 clients = 45% of all 29 interruptions.

## 5. Conclusions
Overall quality of care is strong for ART initiation and VL documentation. Viral load suppression is high and concentrated in Secondary and Tertiary facilities.  
However, retention challenges including ART interruption, poor adherence, and LTFU are also concentrated in these high-volume sites. Baseline CD4 documentation and efficiency in Primary sites remain areas for improvement.

## 6. Recommendations

### Priority 1: Strengthen Retention in Secondary & Tertiary
- Scale up 7-day and 14-day defaulter tracing using expert clients, calls, SMS
- Expand 6MMD and Community ART pickup to reduce congestion and transport barriers
- Provide Enhanced Adherence Counseling + repeat VL for clients with poor adherence

### Priority 2: Close Data Quality Gaps
- Monthly DQA focused on "CD4 At Start" in Secondary and Tertiary facilities
- EMR validation to flag missing baseline CD4
- Re-train clinicians and data clerks on importance of baseline labs

### Priority 3: Improve Efficiency at Primary Level
- Assess referral patterns and strengthen feedback mechanisms
- Implement hub-and-spoke or integrated PHC models to increase client load
- Replicate DSD and staffing best practices from Secondary facilities
